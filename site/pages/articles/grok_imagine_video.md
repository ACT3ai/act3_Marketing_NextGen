# Grok Imagine vs Seedance vs Wan: Cost and Specs

Beyond price, the three differ on the highest resolution they render and the shot lengths they allow, so pick on those facts and settle look and motion by running the same shot on all three.

Below, Grok Imagine, Seedance 2.0, Seedance 2.5 and Wan 2.6 are compared on what can be checked. Realism, motion and style depend on your shot, so instead of ranking them, this page prices a fair three-way test.

## Which one reaches the highest resolution?

Seedance, if you need it. Seedance 2.0 is the only one of the three that renders 4K, and both Seedance 2.0 and 2.5 render 1080p. Wan 2.6 renders 1080p. Grok Imagine tops out at 720p.

At 1080p the prices are far apart. Wan 2.6 costs 97.5 credits a second; Seedance 2.0 1080p costs 243.25, 2.49× as much (243.25 ÷ 97.5); Seedance 2.5 1080p costs 266, 2.73× as much (266 ÷ 97.5).

At 720p the order changes. Seedance 2.0 Mini 720p is 157 credits and Seedance 2.0 Fast 720p is 314, both below Grok Imagine's 364. Seedance 2.0 720p (786) and Seedance 2.5 720p (1,202) are above it.

## Which one allows the shot length you need?

Each model snaps a shot to its nearest allowed length, so length options shape your cut.

* **Grok Imagine, 1–15 s.** The only one below 4 seconds, which suits inserts and quick cutaways.
* **Seedance 2.0, 4–15 s.** Any length in that span.
* **Seedance 2.5, 4–30 s.** The longest single shot of the three.
* **Wan 2.6, 5, 10 or 15 s only.** Three fixed lengths. A 7-second shot snaps to 5 and an 8-second shot snaps to 10.

Cost scales with length; exact prices exist only for 8 seconds. Straight-line estimates: a 2-second Grok Imagine insert is about 364 × 2 ÷ 8 = 91 credits; a 10-second Wan 2.6 shot is about 780 × 10 ÷ 8 = 975 credits; a 30-second Seedance 2.5 1080p take is about 2,128 × 30 ÷ 8 = 7,980 credits. The generate button shows the real figure before anything runs.

## So which trade-offs do the facts support?

* **Cheapest per shot:** Seedance 2.0 Mini, 73 credits at 480p or 157 at 720p.
* **Cheapest at 1080p:** Wan 2.6, 97.5 credits a second, if your cut fits 5, 10 or 15-second shots.
* **Shortest shots:** Grok Imagine, down to 1 second, at 720p.
* **Longest take:** Seedance 2.5, up to 30 seconds.
* **Highest resolution:** Seedance 2.0 at 4K.

## How do you test quality without guessing?

What the three-way test costs:

* **Budget tiers:** Grok Imagine 8 s (364) + Seedance 2.0 Fast 720p 8 s (314) + Wan 2.6 10 s (about 975, estimate) = about 1,653 credits, roughly $3.31.
