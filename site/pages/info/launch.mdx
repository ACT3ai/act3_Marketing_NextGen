# ACT 3 AI Is Here

AI filmmaking at the speed of storytelling. Make [movies](/movies), [TV](/tv), [minidramas](/minidramas) and [videos](/videos).

* **Chat to your AI filmmaker.** You give the input. Your AI filmmaker does the work.
* **Consistency.** Characters, sets and outfits stay the same in every shot.
* **Script to beats, scenes and shots.** Bring the full screenplay. Move a beat and its scenes and shots go with it.
* **Characters and voice.** A character sheet, a voice of their own and a face that holds. Add a voice actor whenever you want one.
* **Storyboards.** A panel for every shot of your movie or video.
* **Sets.** Build a set once, then move the camera inside it.
* **Get the shot right before you pay for video.** Settle it as a storyboard, then a first frame. Video comes last.
* **Teams.** Your whole team works in one project, with full access controls.
* **[MCP](/mcp) and [CLI](/cli).** Run ACT 3 from Claude Code and other AI agents, or from the shell.
