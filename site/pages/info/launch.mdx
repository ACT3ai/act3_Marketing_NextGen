# ACT 3 AI Is Here

ACT 3 AI turns a script into a finished, cinematic video. We are an AI filmmaking platform built for storytellers — solo creators, animators, agencies, marketing teams, and studios — that takes you from the page to the screen inside a single connected workspace.

If you have a story, we want to make it as fast, as affordable, and as beautiful to put on screen as a major-studio production used to be — minus the months, minus the crew costs, minus the tool-switching.

This page introduces ACT 3 AI to people who are new to us. Below are the values we deliver to every customer, in priority order. Each one is unpacked further down.

## 1. Script to Cinematic Video, In One Workspace

You bring the story. ACT 3 AI takes it from screenplay through storyboarding, character creation, set building, shot planning, generation, and final edit — all inside one connected workspace. No more bouncing between a dozen tools that do not talk to each other, no more rebuilding the same character in five different apps, no more losing your context every time you switch programs. The script you wrote on Monday is still wired into the shots you render on Friday.

## 2. Cinematographer-Grade Direction

ACT 3 AI thinks like a film crew, not like a text-to-video toy. We work in the language real productions use: Acts, Scenes, Beats, and Shots — with full control over lighting, lensing, camera moves, blocking, and performance. Every shot is something a cinematographer could sign off on, with the dials a director actually wants in front of them. If you do not speak that language yet, the platform guides you through it. If you already do, you will feel at home immediately.

## 3. Every Top AI Engine, Side-by-Side

The best video model for a quiet dialogue shot is not the best model for a 200-foot battle sequence, and neither is the best model for a stylized animated montage. ACT 3 AI integrates the leading engines — Google Veo 3, Runway, FLUX, ComfyUI, Hunyuan, Wan 2.1 — and lets you pick the right one per shot. You do not have to commit to one vendor's strengths and weaknesses across an entire production. You get the best frame from the best engine, shot by shot, scene by scene.

## 4. Consistent Characters, Sets, and Style

A character who looks like one person in Shot 1 and a different person in Shot 7 breaks the film. ACT 3 AI carries characters, sets, wardrobe, lighting style, and overall visual identity across every shot and every scene of a production. The actor you cast in Scene 1 is the same actor walking through Scene 14. The set you built in Episode 1 is the same set in Episode 6. The work compounds instead of resetting every time you generate.

## 5. From Idea to Finished Video in Hours

A production that used to take a crew of dozens and months of calendar time can run, with ACT 3 AI, in a single afternoon. Storyboards generate in minutes. Shots render in minutes. Edits happen live. The bottleneck moves back where it belongs — to your taste and your storytelling — instead of to logistics, scheduling, and rendering queues.

## 6. Built for Every Creator

ACT 3 AI scales from a solo creator working on a laptop all the way to a studio shipping series and feature work. The same platform powers a first-time storyteller making their first short, a marketing team producing weekly campaigns, and an animation team prototyping a feature. You are not paying for a stripped-down "consumer" tool that you will outgrow, and you are not paying for a bloated enterprise tool that overwhelms a newcomer. One platform, every scale.

## 7. Your Work, Your Assets, Your Pipeline

What you build inside ACT 3 AI is yours. Characters, sets, shots, edits, and final exports flow out to the tools real productions use — Blender sync for full 3D work, an integrated video editor for the final cut, and multi-platform export for delivery to YouTube, social, streaming, or the festival circuit. You are not locked into a closed creative silo. ACT 3 AI is the workspace that makes the production happen; the production itself belongs to you.
