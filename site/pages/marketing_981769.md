# Your Assistant Director Team

**People who produce movies and TV shows with AI filmmaking.**

Beyond the ACT 3 software, you get a dedicated crew of real filmmakers who do the
work for you — working inside the same ACT 3 pipeline you already use, available by
the week. Not just a tool. A team.

## Two packages

| Package | Team | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| **Package A** | **3 team members** | A dedicated team to take on your production, one week at a time. |
| **Package B** | **5 team members** | More hands for bigger slates, tighter deadlines, and parallel shots. |

## How it works

1. **Pick a package** — Package A (3 members) or Package B (5 members).
2. **Book by the week** — A fixed weekly price: 5 working days, 8 hours a day. No per-hour metering.
3. **Get films made** — Your crew produces inside ACT 3; output lands straight in your project.

## Save money

The same production, for far less — because a careful team wastes nothing.

- **Generate less video** — Your team goes over every prompt input and output with great care, so you stop paying for wasted generations — a large saving on video-generation costs.
- **Far smaller team, far lower labor cost** — Produce full shows with a fraction of the headcount you would normally need.
- **Hit every deadline** — Get projects finished on your schedule — even a weekly TV show that has to ship a new episode every single week.
- **Finish in a fraction of the time** — Completing productions faster cuts your cost and puts far more profit in your pocket.

## Higher quality

The quality your investors, executives, and streaming partners expect — and the trust that you are top-tier in the industry.

- **Highly detailed sets & locations** — Chases through sprawling police stations, hospitals, or universities. Build the set to a high standard once, then reuse it across hundreds of episodes — saving many hundreds of hours of labor.
- **Rooms that stay consistent** — Every room and building keeps the same size and every exact detail — even as the camera moves through different angles and transitions between rooms.
- **Continuity down to the details** — Exacting control of details like clothing changes over the course of a story.
- **Characters that evolve** — Experienced with characters changing across a production — aging through stages of life, moving through stages of pregnancy, and changing hairstyle and hair length.

## One person, a full movie or TV show

Keep your own side of the team as lean as you like — even a single director.

- **A cost-efficient team on your side** — Your in-house side can stay small; in many cases a single director can have our team do all of the work.
- **Bullet points in, production out** — The director hands over a simple bullet-point list, and the team turns it around overnight.

## Every night, upgrading an entire movie

Hand us a script and watch it come to life — then refine it day after day.

- **A two-hour movie, ready the next day** — Give us the script for a full two-hour film and have the entire movie created by the following day.
- **A new episode every week** — Have an hour-long TV show finished with a new episode each week. Every day you watch the new version and give input on what to upgrade.
