A set is a place you keep. Not a picture you got once.

Each of these was described one time. The wide picture is the set. Under it, two cameras standing inside it. Pick a set, move the camera, come back to it whenever the story does.

Our secret to saving money

We get the shot right before you pay for the video.

Fewer takes to top quality.

The video is the expensive step, so ACT 3 has you settle the shot where a change costs little: as a storyboard, then as a first frame. When you do generate video, it is already the shot you wanted. That saves you money and time.

Three people. Three time zones. One movie that keeps moving.

Your production designer, your director and your costume designer all work inside the same ACT 3 project, in the same hours or one after another. Nobody waits for a file. Nobody works on an old copy.

  • Admin people and permissions

  • Reviewer watches, never changes

With voice actors. Without. Or both in the same scene.

Every character keeps one voice from the first scene to the last. Cast it from the voice directory and there is no session to book. Hand the part to a voice actor and their takes land right in the shot. Decide role by role.

Without a session

Margie a voice cast from the directory

  • You pick her voice once. It is hers in every scene.
  • Nothing to book and no studio day on the budget.
  • Rewrite her line and it is spoken again, in the same voice.

With a voice actor

Walt performed by your voice actor

  • He records from home, on his own page in your project.
  • Your direction travels with every line.
  • Each take lands in its shot, with the lips matched.
