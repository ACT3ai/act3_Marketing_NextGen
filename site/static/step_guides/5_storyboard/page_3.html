Step 3 of 6

Open the Storyboard from the Editor

Already in the Editor? Type "Show me the storyboard" into the AI chat and click Post. Or click Storyboard on the view switch at the top right.

Continue

The AI chat takes plain requests. Asking for the storyboard switches the Editor to the Storyboard view.

Continue

Two ways in

The storyboard is not only a wizard step. Once your project is open in the Editor, the Storyboard is one view away, any time.

  • Ask the AI chat. Click the chat box under the AI chat, type "Show me the storyboard" and click Post. The chat understands plain words, so "open the storyboard" works too.
  • Use the view switch. At the top right of the Editor, the switch has two sides: the Editor (video) side and Storyboard. Click Storyboard. Click the other side to come back.

If you skipped storyboards in the wizard

The Storyboard still opens. Video shots with no frame yet show "no storyboard yet" and a Generate button, and Generate all at the top draws every missing one for the price it shows. You can also ask the AI chat which shots have no storyboard yet.
