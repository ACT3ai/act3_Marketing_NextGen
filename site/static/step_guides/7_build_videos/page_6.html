One editor for any length

The same timeline holds a two-hour movie or a three-minute video. Zoom out with ZOOM – or Fit to see the whole film; zoom in with + until single shots, and then a few frames, fill the bar. The playhead stays put as you zoom.

Ways to move

  • Click a shot, scene or beat to jump to its start. Drag on the timeline to scrub.
  • Double-click an act, beat or scene to open its own page.
  • Steppers: ‹ Beat ›, ‹ Scene ›, ‹ Shot › in the top bar.
  • Keys: Alt + ← / → steps shots, Shift + ← / → steps scenes.
  • Video | Storyboard at the right of the menu row switches to the storyboard of the same film (see Storyboard).

Play it back

Press play under the video (or Space). The playhead runs forward and the whole page follows it, shot after shot, so you see the cut the way an audience will. Watch for jumps between shots: a change in light, wardrobe or screen direction is your next fix.

The loop you will repeat

Move to a shot, review the take, change the script or first frame if needed, build, and move on. That loop, shot by shot, is how the whole film gets made.
