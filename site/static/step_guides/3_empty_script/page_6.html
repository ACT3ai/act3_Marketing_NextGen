What this page is

The Editor is where the film is made. On a new empty script the SCRIPT pane holds one scene heading, 1. INT. LOCATION - DAY, and an empty line under it. Nothing else is written yet, so there are no scenes or shots to film.

  • SCRIPT (right): your screenplay. Everything else in the Editor is built from it.
  • AI CHAT (middle): your AI filmmaker. Ask it to write or change the script.
  • The timeline (top): Acts, Beats, Scenes and Shots. It fills in as the script grows.
  • THE SHOT (left): the video of the selected shot, once one is built.

The first line is a scene heading, the way a screenplay names a place and a time. Replace LOCATION with your place and DAY with the time, for example INT. KITCHEN - MORNING.

Why you go next to writing

A film needs words before it needs pictures. Next you put the first scene into the script.
