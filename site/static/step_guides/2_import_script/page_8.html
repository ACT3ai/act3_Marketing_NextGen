What these pages are

Can we create storyboards?

A storyboard frame is a still sketch of one shot: the framing, the angle, who is in it. Doing them for the whole film lets you read the film shot by shot before any video is spent. The page shows example frames labelled by shot type (extreme wide, over-the-shoulder, close-up, insert, POV) and the price for your film.

Your decision

  • Create Storyboards ›: draw a frame for every video shot now. A live wall fills with frames as they are drawn. Best when you want to review the whole film's coverage first.
  • Skip ›: go straight to the film build. You can storyboard any scene later from the Editor (guide #5).

Building your film

The last wizard page. AI agents make the character looks, lay out shots for each beat, build sets and draw storyboard frames, with a time estimate on top. A step that fails shows in red with Retry. When the first beat is ready you may open the Editor while the rest keeps building; at 100 percent, CONTINUE › opens the Editor directly.

The Editor

Your film, open: the timeline of acts, beats, scenes and shots across the top, the shot player, the AI chat, the first frame and your script side by side. This is where you build videos and refine every shot.

Where to go from here

The wizard is done. Continue returns you to the Step-by-Step Guide: guide #6 improves a shot's first frame, and guide #7 builds videos and moves through the timeline.
