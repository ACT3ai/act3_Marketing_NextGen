Where you start

Everything begins on the ACT 3 AI website. The Get Started button is on every page of the site, in the top bar, and on the homepage it appears once more under the headline. Both open the same Sign up page in the ACT 3 AI web app.

Your decision

New to ACT 3 AI? Click Get Started. Already have an account? Click Sign in beside it instead; it takes you straight to your Home page, and you can skip ahead to step 3.

Why you go to Sign up next

Your films, scripts, characters and credits belong to an account. Making one takes a single click, so it is the first thing to do.
