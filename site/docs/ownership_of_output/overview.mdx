# Ownership of Output

Much of what you make in ACT 3 AI (images, video, voices, music and text) is generated by AI systems from other companies. Your rights in that output depend on their terms as well as on ACT 3 AI's. This page lists those systems, says in plain words what each one makes for you in ACT 3 AI, and links to the part of each one's terms that covers who owns the output and how it may be used.

We do not restate their terms here. Their documents are the authoritative answer, and they change, so every link goes to the company's live page. Please read them there.

*Last reviewed: November 24, 2026.*

## Copyright in AI-generated material

Whether AI-generated material is protected by copyright is a separate legal question from these contracts. See:

* [U.S. Copyright Office: Copyright and Artificial Intelligence (Part 2 Copyrightability report, registration guidance)](https://www.copyright.gov/ai/)
* [U.S. Copyright Office: Copyright Registration Guidance for works containing AI-generated material (March 16, 2023)](https://www.copyright.gov/ai/ai_policy_guidance.pdf)
* [Thaler v. Perlmutter, D.C. Circuit opinion (March 18, 2025)](https://media.cadc.uscourts.gov/opinions/docs/2025/03/23-5233.pdf)
* [UK Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988, section 9 (computer-generated works)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/48/section/9)
* [EU AI Act Article 50: transparency and labelling of AI-generated content (AI Act Service Desk)](https://ai-act-service-desk.ec.europa.eu/en/ai-act/article-50)
* [Beijing Internet Court judgment on an AI-generated image, Li v. Liu (2023), English translation](https://english.bjinternetcourt.gov.cn/2023-12/28/c_688.htm)
