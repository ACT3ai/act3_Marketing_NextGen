# Roadmap

Here's what we're building next at ACT 3 AI.

## In Progress

- **Real-time collaboration** — Work on productions with your team simultaneously
- **Custom model fine-tuning** — Train your own actors and styles on proprietary models
- **Audio generation** — AI-generated music and sound design matched to your shots

## Coming Soon

- **Storyboard generator** — AI-drawn storyboard frames before you commit to video generation
- **Scene continuity tracking** — Automatic continuity checks between shots
- **Script coverage** — AI script notes and coverage reports
- **Premiere Pro plugin** — Direct export to Premiere Pro timeline

## Future

- **On-set AI assistant** — Real-time shot suggestions during live production
- **Real-time preview** — Low-res instant preview before full generation
- **3D scene reconstruction** — Convert generated video into navigable 3D environments

## Recently Shipped

- ✅ Google Veo 3 integration
- ✅ Wan 2.1 support
- ✅ Blender sync v2
- ✅ Multi-take comparison view
- ✅ Aspect ratio templates (9:16, 1:1, 16:9, 2.39:1)
