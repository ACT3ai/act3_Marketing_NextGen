# Your First AI Video

In this tutorial, we'll create a short 30-second video from scratch using ACT 3 AI.

## What We'll Build

A dramatic 30-second opening sequence: a lone figure walks into an abandoned warehouse at night.

**Scene:** Night. Exterior and interior of an abandoned industrial warehouse.
**Mood:** Tense, cinematic, noir-influenced.
**Shots:** 4 shots, approximately 7-8 seconds each.

## Step 1: Create the Project

1. Log in at [act3ai.com](https://act3ai.com)
2. Click **New Project**
3. Name it "Warehouse Scene Tutorial"
4. Select **16:9 widescreen**
5. Click **Create**

## Step 2: Define the Scene

In the Script editor, create one scene:

```
EXT. ABANDONED WAREHOUSE - NIGHT

A lone figure approaches an industrial warehouse. 
Streetlights cast long shadows. The building is dark.
```

ACT 3 AI will parse this automatically.

## Step 3: Define Your Shots

For this scene, add 4 shots:

**Shot 1 — Establishing Drone**
- Type: Extreme wide
- Camera: Drone
- Movement: Push in from above, descending
- Lens: 24mm
- Lighting: Night, available streetlight, high contrast

**Shot 2 — Figure Approaching**
- Type: Wide
- Camera: Steadicam
- Movement: Tracking with figure
- Lens: 35mm
- Lighting: Backlit by distant streetlight

**Shot 3 — Detail — Hands on Door**
- Type: Extreme close-up
- Camera: Locked-off
- Movement: Static
- Lens: 85mm
- Lighting: Low key, single practical source

**Shot 4 — Interior Reveal**
- Type: Wide
- Camera: Dolly
- Movement: Push in
- Lens: 24mm
- Lighting: Darkness with shaft of light from doorway

## Step 4: Generate

Select all 4 shots, choose **Veo 3**, and click **Generate All**.

Wait for generation to complete (typically 2-3 minutes per shot).

## Step 5: Assemble

Open the **Timeline Editor**. Drag your 4 shots in order. Add a subtle audio track —
ambient industrial sounds and low tension music work well for this scene.

Export at 1080p for web delivery.

**Congratulations — you've created your first AI film scene!**
