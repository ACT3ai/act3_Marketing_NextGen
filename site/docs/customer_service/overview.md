# Customer Service

We're here to help. Whether you have a question, have run into a problem, or just want to share an idea, we'd love to hear from you.

## Email us

ACT 3 AI provides customer service by email. Please write to us at:

**[support@act3ai.com](mailto:support@act3ai.com)**

A real person on our team reads every message and will get back to you as soon as we can.

## What we can help with

- **Your account** — signing in, your profile, and your organization
- **Billing and credits** — plans, payments, invoices, and credit balances
- **Making your film** — scripts, beats, scenes, shots, characters, sets, and video generation
- **Something not working** — errors, stuck jobs, or results that don't look right
- **Ideas and feedback** — features you'd like to see, or anything we could do better

## Help us help you faster

When you email us, it helps to include:

- The email address you use to sign in to ACT 3 AI
- The name of the project you're working on, if it's about a project
- What you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead
- A screenshot or the exact error message, if you saw one

Don't worry if you don't have all of these — just write to us and we'll take it from there.

## Thank you

Thank you for making films with ACT 3 AI. Every message helps us make the product better, and we look forward to hearing from you.
