# Welcome to ACT 3 AI

ACT 3 AI is the world's first **AI Showrunner platform** — a complete end-to-end production pipeline
that takes your screenplay from script to finished video.

## What is ACT 3 AI?

ACT 3 AI combines the power of the world's best AI video generation models — Google Veo 3, Runway,
FLUX, ComfyUI, Hunyuan, and Wan 2.1 — with a professional cinematography layer that understands
storytelling.

The result: you write the story, ACT 3 AI produces it.

## Core Concepts

ACT 3 AI is built around the structure of film production:

- **Beats** — The emotional moments that drive your story forward
- **Scenes** — A continuous action in a single location
- **Shots** — Individual camera setups within a scene

This structure maps directly to how professional filmmakers work — and directly to how AI video
generation models receive instructions.

## Key Features

| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| Script Parser | Break any screenplay into acts, scenes, beats, and shots |
| AI Cinematography | Professional camera, lens, lighting, and movement per shot |
| Multi-Model Generation | Veo 3, Runway, FLUX, ComfyUI, Hunyuan, Wan 2.1 |
| Actor Management | Consistent characters across scenes with lip sync & mocap |
| Video Editor | Timeline editor with multi-platform export |
| Blender Sync | 3D compositing and VFX via Blender integration |
