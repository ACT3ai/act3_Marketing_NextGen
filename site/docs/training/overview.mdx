# Training

ACT 3 AI is built on AI systems from a number of other companies. This page lists the ones ACT 3 AI may use, says in plain words which part of ACT 3 AI uses each one, and links to that company's own documents about how it uses the data sent to it, including whether it trains on it.

We do not restate their terms here. Their documents are the authoritative answer, and they change, so every link goes to the company's live page. Please read them there.

*Last reviewed: November 24, 2026.*
