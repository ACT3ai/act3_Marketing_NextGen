# Quickstart Guide

This guide walks you through creating your first AI-generated video with ACT3 AI.

## Step 1: Create an Account

Visit [act3ai.com](https://act3ai.com) and sign up for a free account. No credit card required.

## Step 2: Create a New Project

From your dashboard, click **New Project** and give it a name. Choose your target aspect ratio
(16:9 for widescreen, 9:16 for vertical/mobile).

## Step 3: Add Your Script

You can either:
- **Paste your screenplay** directly into the script editor
- **Start from scratch** and write scene descriptions
- **Use a template** from our library of example scripts

ACT3 AI will automatically parse your script into acts, scenes, and beats.

## Step 4: Review Your Shot Breakdown

ACT3 AI generates a shot breakdown for each scene. Each shot includes:
- Camera angle and type
- Lens focal length
- Camera movement
- Lighting setup
- Subject framing

Review and adjust any shots that don't match your vision.

## Step 5: Generate Video

Select one or more shots and click **Generate**. Choose your AI model:
- **Veo 3** — Best quality, most realistic
- **Runway** — Fast, great for stylized content
- **ComfyUI/FLUX** — Local rendering, full control

ACT3 AI sends your shot descriptions to the model and returns the generated video.

## Step 6: Assemble and Export

Drag your generated shots into the timeline editor. Add audio, transitions, and color
grading. Export in your desired format.
